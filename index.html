Read this before you scoop.

Caffeine adds up.
Plenty of tubs run 300+ mg per serving — before you count your morning coffee. The FDA cites about 400 mg a day as a level generally not associated with negative effects in healthy adults. Know your total.
Same scoop, different human.
Body size, sleep, medications, and plain genetics change how the same serving lands. Start with a half serving when you try anything new.
Talk to your doctor.
Especially if you're pregnant or nursing, have a medical condition, or take medication. Nothing on this site is medical advice.
