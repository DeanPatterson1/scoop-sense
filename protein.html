Protein per serving, protein-per-scoop percentage, source, and sweetener for every powder we track — taken from the manufacturer's label.

A protein label tells you two numbers that matter together: grams of protein and grams per scoop. A 24 g serving from a 30 g scoop is a leaner formula than 24 g from a 40 g scoop — the rest is flavoring, fats, and carbs. Every entry below logs both, plus the protein source and the sweetener used.
