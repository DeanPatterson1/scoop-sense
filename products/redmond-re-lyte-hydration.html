Product details

What is Redmond Re-Lyte Hydration?

Sodium-forward at 810 mg with 400 mg potassium and zero sugar; 60 servings per tub makes it one of the cheapest per-serving options in the high-sodium tier. It is one of the hydration mixes in the Scoop Sense database. Everything on this page comes from the manufacturer's supplement facts panel as of July 2026 — never from the marketing page.

Key ingredients & studied doses
  • Sodium (from Real Salt) · 810 mg. Sodium is the primary electrolyte lost in sweat and is studied for its role in fluid balance during prolonged sweating.
  • Potassium · 400 mg. Potassium contributes to normal muscle function and works with sodium in fluid balance.
  • Magnesium · 50 mg. Magnesium plays a role in normal muscle and nerve function.
Cautions
  • 810 mg sodium per serving — significant if you watch sodium intake
  • Talk to your doctor if you take blood-pressure medication
  • Hydration mixes are formulated for sweat loss, not everyday sipping
Flavors & sweetener

Flavored options like Lemon Lime plus an Unflavored version, sweetened with stevia leaf extract and zero sugar.

Who it's best for

Heavy sweaters and low-carb dieters — this is a sodium-forward mix. A reasonable pick when cost per serving matters. Derived from the label figures above — not medical advice.

Common questions about Redmond Re-Lyte Hydration

How much sodium is in Redmond Re-Lyte Hydration?

810 mg per serving, with no sugar. Sodium is the main electrolyte lost in sweat, but needs vary widely with sweat rate and diet — and daily sodium from food counts toward the same total.

Does Redmond Re-Lyte Hydration disclose every dose?

Yes — the label lists an individual amount for each ingredient, with no proprietary blends. That is what the "label fully disclosed" tag on Scoop Sense means, and it is what lets each dose be compared against the amounts used in published research.
