Common questions about Evlution Nutrition BCAA Energy

How much caffeine is in Evlution Nutrition BCAA Energy?

110 mg per full labeled serving — roughly 1.2 small cups of coffee. The FDA cites about 400 mg per day as an amount generally not associated with negative effects in healthy adults, and coffee, tea, and energy drinks count toward the same total.

Is Evlution Nutrition BCAA Energy an EAA or a BCAA product?

BCAA-only: it supplies the three branched-chain aminos (5 g per serving) but not the other six essential amino acids that full-EAA formulas include.

Why does Evlution Nutrition BCAA Energy make my skin tingle?

The 500 mg of beta-alanine. The prickling (paresthesia) in the face, neck, and hands is generally considered harmless, is dose-dependent, and fades on its own — typically within about an hour. Most beta-alanine studies use 3.2 g per day.

What does the proprietary blend in Evlution Nutrition BCAA Energy hide?

The BCAA 2:1:1 blend lists 5 g as a combined total. A blend total tells you the sum of its ingredients, not the individual doses inside it — which is why blend entries can't be checked against studied amounts.
