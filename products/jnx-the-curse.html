Product details

What is JNX Sports The Curse!?

A long-running budget bestseller that still relies on two proprietary blends, so exact beta-alanine, creatine, and citrulline doses aren't disclosed; the 155 mg caffeine figure is directly confirmed on the label and sits at the low-moderate end for the category. It sits in the moderate stim tier of the Scoop Sense pre-workout database, which spans 0–400 mg of caffeine per serving. Everything on this page comes from the manufacturer's supplement facts panel as of July 2026 — never from the marketing page.

Key ingredients & studied doses
  • Caffeine Anhydrous · 155 mg. A moderate single-scoop dose, roughly one to two cups of coffee; on the lower end for a stimulant pre-workout.
  • Energizing Muscle Fuel Blend (Beta-Alanine, Creatine Monohydrate, Citric Acid) · 3,000 mg (blend). Proprietary blend; the individual beta-alanine and creatine amounts aren't disclosed, so neither can be confirmed against the roughly 3.2 g beta-alanine or 3-5 g creatine doses used in most research.
  • Amplifier Blend (L-Citrulline, L-Arginine Alpha-Ketoglutarate) · 900 mg (blend). Well under the 6-8 g of citrulline alone used in most pump research; the proprietary format hides how much of the 900 mg is citrulline versus arginine AKG.
Cautions
  • Two proprietary blends (Energizing Muscle Fuel Blend and Amplifier Blend) hide the individual beta-alanine, creatine, citrulline, and arginine AKG amounts.
  • All figures above are per single 5.1 g scoop; the label allows up to 3 scoops, which would triple the caffeine to about 465 mg.
  • Beta-alanine (undisclosed dose within the blend) may cause a harmless tingling sensation if dosed near typical study levels.
Flavors & sweetener

One of the largest flavor lineups in the category, with about 17 options (Blue Raspberry, Green Apple, Watermelon, Fruit Punch, Dark Grape, Pina Colada, Mango Chili and more), sweetened with sucralose and acesulfame potassium.

Who it's best for

Most regular gym-goers — the caffeine dose is in the range of about two cups of coffee. A reasonable pick when cost per serving matters. Skip it if you want every individual dose disclosed on the label. Derived from the label figures above — not medical advice.

Common questions about JNX Sports The Curse!

How much caffeine is in JNX Sports The Curse!?

155 mg per full labeled serving — roughly 1.6 small cups of coffee. Within the Scoop Sense pre-workout database (0–400 mg per serving) that places it in the moderate stim tier. The FDA cites about 400 mg per day as an amount generally not associated with negative effects in healthy adults, and coffee, tea, and energy drinks count toward the same total.

Why does JNX Sports The Curse! make my skin tingle?

The 3,000 mg (blend) of beta-alanine. The prickling (paresthesia) in the face, neck, and hands is generally considered harmless, is dose-dependent, and fades on its own — typically within about an hour. Most beta-alanine studies use 3.2 g per day.

What does the proprietary blend in JNX Sports The Curse! hide?

The Energizing Muscle Fuel Blend (Beta-Alanine, Creatine Monohydrate, Citric Acid) lists 3,000 mg (blend) as a combined total. A blend total tells you the sum of its ingredients, not the individual doses inside it — which is why blend entries can't be checked against studied amounts.

Do the numbers for JNX Sports The Curse! assume one scoop or two?

All figures on this page are per full labeled serving. All figures above are per single 5.1 g scoop; the label allows up to 3 scoops, which would triple the caffeine to about 465 mg..
