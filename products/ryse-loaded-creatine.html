Product details

Where this label sits

RYSE Loaded Creatine is one of the creatine products in the Scoop Sense database. Every active ingredient on the panel is listed with its own dose — nothing is hidden inside a blend total. It runs 30 servings per container at the full labeled serving. Everything on this page comes from the manufacturer's supplement facts panel as of July 2026 — never from the marketing page.

Key ingredients & studied doses
  • Creatine Monohydrate · 4 g. The primary, most-studied form of creatine in the blend, supporting muscle strength and power output.
  • Creatine MagnaPower (Magnesium Creatine Chelate) · 1.5 g. A patented magnesium-bound creatine form included alongside monohydrate and HCl in this three-form blend.
  • Creatine HCl · 750 mg. A more soluble creatine salt form dosed in milligrams rather than grams.
  • Betaine Anhydrous · 2.5 g. Betaine has been studied for supporting muscular endurance and power during training.

Common questions about RYSE Loaded Creatine

Do I need a loading phase with RYSE Loaded Creatine?

The loading question does not transfer to this form. Saturation timelines come from research on 3–5 g of creatine monohydrate daily; blend has no established studied dose of its own, so there is no figure to load toward. Treat the label's 6.25 g as the brand's recommendation rather than a researched one.
