Where this label sits

Insane Labz Psychotic sits in the high stim tier of the Scoop Sense pre-workout database, which spans 0–400 mg of caffeine per serving. The label uses at least one proprietary blend, so a blend total stands in place of the individual amounts inside it. It runs 35 servings per container at the full labeled serving. Everything on this page comes from the manufacturer's supplement facts panel as of September 2026 — never from the marketing page.

Key ingredients & studied doses
  • Psychotic Blend · 4,459 mg total. Eight ingredients share one number — beta-alanine, creatine monohydrate, caffeine anhydrous, AMPiberry, OxyGold fulvic acid, DMAE, Rauwolfia vomitoria and Huperzia serrata — so only the blend total can be compared to anything.
  • Caffeine · 400 mg. Disclosed by Insane Labz outside the Supplement Facts panel rather than inside it; 400 mg is the FDA's daily reference amount for healthy adults, reached here in one 6.2 g scoop.
  • Beta-Alanine · Undisclosed (inside the 4,459 mg blend). Cannot be compared to the 3.2 g/day used in beta-alanine research because the label never prints the amount; it is listed first in the blend, so it is the largest single component.
  • Creatine Monohydrate · Undisclosed (inside the 4,459 mg blend). The whole blend is 4,459 mg and 400 mg of that is caffeine, so creatine and beta-alanine together have under 4.1 g to share — the 3-5 g daily amount used in creatine research is not reachable alongside a meaningful beta-alanine dose.

Common questions about Insane Labz Psychotic

How much caffeine is in Insane Labz Psychotic?

400 mg per full labeled serving — roughly 4.2 small cups of coffee. Within the Scoop Sense pre-workout database (0–400 mg per serving) that places it in the high stim tier. The FDA cites about 400 mg per day as an amount generally not associated with negative effects in healthy adults, and coffee, tea, and energy drinks count toward the same total.

Why does Insane Labz Psychotic make my skin tingle?

The Undisclosed (inside the 4,459 mg blend) of beta-alanine. The prickling (paresthesia) in the face, neck, and hands is generally considered harmless, is dose-dependent, and fades on its own — typically within about an hour. Most beta-alanine studies use 3.2 g per day.

What does the proprietary blend in Insane Labz Psychotic hide?

The Psychotic Blend lists 4,459 mg total as a combined total. A blend total tells you the sum of its ingredients, not the individual doses inside it — which is why blend entries can't be checked against studied amounts.

Do the numbers for Insane Labz Psychotic assume one scoop or two?

All figures on this page are per full labeled serving. 400 mg caffeine in a single 6.2 g scoop; the label's own directions allow one serving in 24 hours, say not to take it within 4 hours of bedtime, and say not to add caffeine from any other source.
