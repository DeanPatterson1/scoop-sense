Product details

What is Ultima Ultima Replenisher Electrolyte Mix?

Potassium- and magnesium-leaning profile with only 55 mg sodium — an everyday flavored-water mix rather than a sweat-replacement formula. It is one of the hydration mixes in the Scoop Sense database. Everything on this page comes from the manufacturer's supplement facts panel as of July 2026 — never from the marketing page.

Key ingredients & studied doses
  • Potassium · 250 mg. Potassium contributes to normal muscle function and fluid balance.
  • Magnesium (magnesium citrate) · 100 mg. Magnesium plays a role in normal muscle and nerve function.
  • Vitamin C · 100 mg. Vitamin C contributes to normal immune function.
  • Sodium · 55 mg. Sodium is studied for its role in fluid balance, though this dose is far below sweat-replacement levels.
Cautions
  • Only 55 mg sodium per serving — light relative to products built for heavy sweat loss
  • Talk to your doctor if you take medication affecting potassium or magnesium levels
Flavors & sweetener

Wide zero-sugar flavor range (Grape, Orange, Raspberry, Watermelon and more) sweetened with organic stevia leaf extract.

Who it's best for

Lighter sessions and everyday training — the sodium load is moderate. Doses are mild enough to suit a first tub. Derived from the label figures above — not medical advice.

How much sodium is in Ultima Ultima Replenisher Electrolyte Mix?

55 mg per serving, with no sugar. Sodium is the main electrolyte lost in sweat, but needs vary widely with sweat rate and diet — and daily sodium from food counts toward the same total.
