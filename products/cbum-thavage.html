Key ingredients & studied doses
  • Caffeine (260 mg anhydrous + 64 mg di-caffeine malate) · 305 mg total. A high combined dose at the full two-scoop serving, well above the ~200mg used in most single-dose caffeine studies; the di-caffeine malate portion contributes roughly 45mg of the total and is marketed as a slower-releasing form.
  • L-Citrulline · 6 g. Within the 6-8g range shown to support nitric-oxide-driven blood flow and pump in citrulline research.
  • Beta-Alanine · 3.2 g. Matches the ~3.2g daily dose used in most beta-alanine muscle-carnosine loading studies.
  • Nitrosigine · 1.5 g. Matches the 1.5g dose used in Nitrosigine's published pump and cognitive-performance research.

Common questions about Raw Nutrition (CBUM Series) Thavage

How much caffeine is in Raw Nutrition (CBUM Series) Thavage?

305 mg per full labeled serving — roughly 3.2 small cups of coffee. Within the Scoop Sense pre-workout database (0–400 mg per serving) that places it in the high stim tier. The FDA cites about 400 mg per day as an amount generally not associated with negative effects in healthy adults, and coffee, tea, and energy drinks count toward the same total.

Why does Raw Nutrition (CBUM Series) Thavage make my skin tingle?

The 3.2 g of beta-alanine. The prickling (paresthesia) in the face, neck, and hands is generally considered harmless, is dose-dependent, and fades on its own — typically within about an hour. Most beta-alanine studies use 3.2 g per day.

Does Raw Nutrition (CBUM Series) Thavage disclose every dose?

Yes — the label lists an individual amount for each ingredient, with no proprietary blends. That is what the "label fully disclosed" tag on Scoop Sense means, and it is what lets each dose be compared against the amounts used in published research.

Do the numbers for Raw Nutrition (CBUM Series) Thavage assume one scoop or two?

All figures on this page are per full labeled serving. 305mg caffeine is the full two-scoop (26.8g) dose; one scoop (13.4g) provides roughly half that for those titrating up..
