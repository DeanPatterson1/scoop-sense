Common questions about MuscleTech Nitro-Tech 100% Whey Gold

How much of a MuscleTech Nitro-Tech 100% Whey Gold scoop is actually protein?

24 g of protein from a 33 g serving — about 73%. The rest is flavoring, fats, carbs, and whatever else the label lists. A higher percentage means less filler per scoop.

What does the proprietary blend in MuscleTech Nitro-Tech 100% Whey Gold hide?

The Whey Protein Blend (Concentrate, Isolate, Peptides) lists 24 g as a combined total. A blend total tells you the sum of its ingredients, not the individual doses inside it — which is why blend entries can't be checked against studied amounts.
