Product details

Where this label sits

MuscleTech Amino Build is one of the amino formulas in the Scoop Sense database. Every active ingredient on the panel is listed with its own dose — nothing is hidden inside a blend total. It runs 40 servings per container at the full labeled serving. Everything on this page comes from the manufacturer's supplement facts panel as of July 2026 — never from the marketing page.

Key ingredients & studied doses
  • L-Leucine · 4 g. Dosed at the level used in university research tied to strength gains after training.
  • L-Isoleucine and L-Valine · 3 g combined. The other two branched-chain aminos, rounding out a leucine-heavy 7 g BCAA total.
  • Betaine anhydrous · 2.5 g. Studied for supporting power output and lean mass gains during resistance training.
  • Taurine · 1 g. An amino acid studied for supporting hydration and exercise performance.
Flavors & sweetener

Tropical Twist and Strawberry Watermelon flavors, caffeine-free.

Who it's best for

A BCAA-only formula — three of the nine essential amino acids, which suits taste-driven intra-workout sipping more than protein replacement. Derived from the label figures above — not medical advice.

Common questions about MuscleTech Amino Build

Is MuscleTech Amino Build an EAA or a BCAA product?

BCAA-only: it supplies the three branched-chain aminos (7 g per serving) but not the other six essential amino acids that full-EAA formulas include.

Does MuscleTech Amino Build disclose every dose?

Yes — the label lists an individual amount for each ingredient, with no proprietary blends. That is what the "label fully disclosed" tag on Scoop Sense means, and it is what lets each dose be compared against the amounts used in published research.
