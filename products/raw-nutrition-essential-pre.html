Where this label sits

Raw Nutrition Essential Pre-Workout sits in the moderate stim tier of the Scoop Sense pre-workout database, which spans 0–400 mg of caffeine per serving. Every active ingredient on the panel is listed with its own dose — nothing is hidden inside a blend total. It runs 30 servings per container at the full labeled serving. Everything on this page comes from the manufacturer's supplement facts panel as of September 2026 — never from the marketing page.

Key ingredients & studied doses
  • L-Citrulline · 4 g. Below the 6-8g of straight citrulline used in most pump research, though comparable to what a 6-8g citrulline malate dose actually delivers.
  • Beta-Alanine · 3.2 g. Exactly the 3.2g daily amount used in most beta-alanine muscle-carnosine loading studies.
  • Caffeine · 200 mg. Matches the ~200mg single dose used in most caffeine performance research; here it comes from green coffee bean extract rather than caffeine anhydrous.
  • L-Tyrosine · 2 g. A real dose by pre-workout standards, but acute tyrosine studies typically use far more - on the order of 100-150mg per kg of bodyweight.

Common questions about Raw Nutrition Essential Pre-Workout

How much caffeine is in Raw Nutrition Essential Pre-Workout?

200 mg per full labeled serving — roughly 2.1 small cups of coffee. Within the Scoop Sense pre-workout database (0–400 mg per serving) that places it in the moderate stim tier. The FDA cites about 400 mg per day as an amount generally not associated with negative effects in healthy adults, and coffee, tea, and energy drinks count toward the same total.

Why does Raw Nutrition Essential Pre-Workout make my skin tingle?

The 3.2 g of beta-alanine. The prickling (paresthesia) in the face, neck, and hands is generally considered harmless, is dose-dependent, and fades on its own — typically within about an hour. Most beta-alanine studies use 3.2 g per day.

Does Raw Nutrition Essential Pre-Workout disclose every dose?

Yes — the label lists an individual amount for each ingredient, with no proprietary blends. That is what the "label fully disclosed" tag on Scoop Sense means, and it is what lets each dose be compared against the amounts used in published research.

Do the numbers for Raw Nutrition Essential Pre-Workout assume one scoop or two?

All figures on this page are per full labeled serving. Every figure here is one 13.3-13.5g scoop (the scoop weight varies slightly by flavor) and the label offers no larger serving column..
