Product details

Where this label sits

BPN Whey Protein is one of the protein powders in the Scoop Sense database. The label uses at least one proprietary blend, so a blend total stands in place of the individual amounts inside it. It runs 27 servings per container at the full labeled serving. Everything on this page comes from the manufacturer's supplement facts panel as of July 2026 — never from the marketing page.

Key ingredients & studied doses
  • Whey Protein Concentrate + Micellar Casein blend · 25 g protein (34.1 g scoop). Pairs a fast-digesting dairy protein with a slow-digesting one, studied for supporting muscle protein synthesis and post-exercise recovery.
  • Calcium · 160 mg. Mineral involved in normal muscle contraction and bone maintenance.
  • Potassium · 160 mg. Electrolyte involved in normal fluid balance and muscle function.
Flavors & sweetener

Offered in eight-plus flavors including Vanilla, Fudge Chocolate, and Cinnamon Roll, all naturally sweetened with stevia (Reb-M) and monk fruit rather than sugar.

Common questions about BPN Whey Protein

How much of a BPN Whey Protein scoop is actually protein?

25 g of protein from a 34 g serving — about 74%. The rest is flavoring, fats, carbs, and whatever else the label lists. A higher percentage means less filler per scoop.

What does the proprietary blend in BPN Whey Protein hide?

The Whey Protein Concentrate + Micellar Casein blend lists 25 g protein (34.1 g scoop) as a combined total. A blend total tells you the sum of its ingredients, not the individual doses inside it — which is why blend entries can't be checked against studied amounts.
