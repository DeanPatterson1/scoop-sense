Product details

Where this label sits

Premier Protein 100% Whey Protein Powder is one of the protein powders in the Scoop Sense database. The label uses at least one proprietary blend, so a blend total stands in place of the individual amounts inside it. It runs 17 servings per container at the full labeled serving. Everything on this page comes from the manufacturer's supplement facts panel as of September 2026 — never from the marketing page.

Key ingredients & studied doses
  • Whey protein blend (whey protein isolate, whey protein concentrate) · 30 g. Isolate is listed first and the tub states 100% of the protein comes from whey; 30 g per serving sits at the upper end of the 20-40 g per-serving range used in muscle protein synthesis research.
  • Sucralose and acesulfame potassium · amount not stated on label. The two high-intensity sweeteners carrying the flavor; the panel shows 1 g total sugars and 0 g added sugars, so sweetness comes from these rather than from sugar.
  • Soluble corn fiber · amount not stated on label. Listed inside the "2% or less" group, and the panel still prints 0 g dietary fiber, so the amount is too small to compare with published fiber intakes.
Flavors & sweetener

Six flavors in the classic powder line — Vanilla Milkshake, Chocolate Milkshake, Café Latte, Strawberry Milkshake, Chocolate Peanut Butter and Salted Caramel Truffle — plus a separate Protein Plus Fiber powder in Chocolate and Vanilla; sold as a 17-serving tub and a 29-serving one, and the figures here are the 17-serving Vanilla Milkshake panel; all sweetened with acesulfame potassium and sucralose, with no added sugar on the panel.

Who it's best for

A standard-ratio powder — fine as a food supplement when total daily protein is what matters. A sensible first tub — nothing on the panel that catches a new user out. Skip it if you want every individual dose disclosed on the label. Derived from the label figures above — not medical advice.

Common questions about Premier Protein 100% Whey Protein Powder

How much of a Premier Protein 100% Whey Protein Powder scoop is actually protein?

30 g of protein from a 39 g serving — about 77%. The rest is flavoring, fats, carbs, and whatever else the label lists. A higher percentage means less filler per scoop.

What does the proprietary blend in Premier Protein 100% Whey Protein Powder hide?

The Whey protein blend (whey protein isolate, whey protein concentrate) lists 30 g as a combined total. A blend total tells you the sum of its ingredients, not the individual doses inside it — which is why blend entries can't be checked against studied amounts.
