Common questions about Legion Pulse

How much caffeine is in Legion Pulse?

350 mg per full labeled serving — roughly 3.7 small cups of coffee. Within the Scoop Sense pre-workout database (0–400 mg per serving) that places it in the high stim tier. The FDA cites about 400 mg per day as an amount generally not associated with negative effects in healthy adults, and coffee, tea, and energy drinks count toward the same total.

Why does Legion Pulse make my skin tingle?

The 3.6 g of beta-alanine. The prickling (paresthesia) in the face, neck, and hands is generally considered harmless, is dose-dependent, and fades on its own — typically within about an hour. Most beta-alanine studies use 3.2 g per day.

Does Legion Pulse disclose every dose?

Yes — the label lists an individual amount for each ingredient, with no proprietary blends. That is what the "label fully disclosed" tag on Scoop Sense means, and it is what lets each dose be compared against the amounts used in published research.

Do the numbers for Legion Pulse assume one scoop or two?

All figures on this page are per full labeled serving. 350 mg caffeine is the FULL two-scoop dose — the label's own default for normal training is one scoop (175 mg).
