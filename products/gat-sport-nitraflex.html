Common questions about GAT Sport Nitraflex Advanced

How much caffeine is in GAT Sport Nitraflex Advanced?

325 mg per full labeled serving — roughly 3.4 small cups of coffee. Within the Scoop Sense pre-workout database (0–400 mg per serving) that places it in the high stim tier. The FDA cites about 400 mg per day as an amount generally not associated with negative effects in healthy adults, and coffee, tea, and energy drinks count toward the same total.

Why does GAT Sport Nitraflex Advanced make my skin tingle?

The 1.5 g of beta-alanine. The prickling (paresthesia) in the face, neck, and hands is generally considered harmless, is dose-dependent, and fades on its own — typically within about an hour. Most beta-alanine studies use 3.2 g per day.

What does the proprietary blend in GAT Sport Nitraflex Advanced hide?

The blend lists one combined total. A blend total tells you the sum of its ingredients, not the individual doses inside it — which is why blend entries can't be checked against studied amounts.

Do the numbers for GAT Sport Nitraflex Advanced assume one scoop or two?

All figures on this page are per full labeled serving. The label's own limits: start at half a scoop, no more than one scoop in 24 hours, none within 5 hours of bedtime, and a week off after eight weeks.
