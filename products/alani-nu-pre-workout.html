What is Alani Nu Pre-Workout?

Disclosed label at 200 mg of caffeine; citrulline reaches the studied range while beta-alanine and tyrosine sit below it. It sits in the moderate stim tier of the Scoop Sense pre-workout database, which spans 0–400 mg of caffeine per serving. Everything on this page comes from the manufacturer's supplement facts panel as of July 2026 — never from the marketing page.

Key ingredients & studied doses
  • L-Citrulline · 6 g. At the low end of the 6–8 g range used in most citrulline studies.
  • Beta-Alanine · 1.6 g. Half the 3.2 g/day amount most beta-alanine studies use.
  • L-Tyrosine · 500 mg. Under the amounts used in most tyrosine research on focus during stress.
Cautions
  • 200 mg caffeine — count the coffee you've already had that day
  • Beta-alanine sits at half the studied amount, so effects are modest

Common questions about Alani Nu Pre-Workout

How much caffeine is in Alani Nu Pre-Workout?

200 mg per full labeled serving — roughly 2.1 small cups of coffee. Within the Scoop Sense pre-workout database (0–400 mg per serving) that places it in the moderate stim tier. The FDA cites about 400 mg per day as an amount generally not associated with negative effects in healthy adults, and coffee, tea, and energy drinks count toward the same total.

Why does Alani Nu Pre-Workout make my skin tingle?

The 1.6 g of beta-alanine. The prickling (paresthesia) in the face, neck, and hands is generally considered harmless, is dose-dependent, and fades on its own — typically within about an hour. Most beta-alanine studies use 3.2 g per day.

Does Alani Nu Pre-Workout disclose every dose?

Yes — the label lists an individual amount for each ingredient, with no proprietary blends. That is what the "label fully disclosed" tag on Scoop Sense means, and it is what lets each dose be compared against the amounts used in published research.
