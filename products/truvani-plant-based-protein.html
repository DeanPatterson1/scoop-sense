Common questions about Truvani Plant Based Protein Powder

How much of a Truvani Plant Based Protein Powder scoop is actually protein?

20 g of protein from a 29 g serving — about 69%. The rest is flavoring, fats, carbs, and whatever else the label lists. A higher percentage means less filler per scoop.

What does the proprietary blend in Truvani Plant Based Protein Powder hide?

The Plant protein blend (organic pea protein, organic pumpkin seed protein, organic chia seed protein) lists 20 g as a combined total. A blend total tells you the sum of its ingredients, not the individual doses inside it — which is why blend entries can't be checked against studied amounts.
