Product details

What is Thorne Amino Complex?

Clinically oriented EAA profile with 3.8 g of EAAs and 1.25 g leucine per 7.6 g scoop, carrying NSF Certified for Sport status at a premium price. It is one of the amino formulas in the Scoop Sense database. Everything on this page comes from the manufacturer's supplement facts panel as of July 2026 — never from the marketing page.

Key ingredients & studied doses
  • L-Leucine · 1.25 g. The essential amino acid most studied for switching on muscle protein synthesis.
  • EAA spectrum · 3.8 g total. All nine essential amino acids individually dosed, supporting protein balance in muscle.
  • L-Lysine · 650 mg. Essential amino acid the body uses in building muscle and connective tissue proteins.
Cautions
  • Doses per amino are lighter than most gym-brand EAA powders
  • Amino drinks supplement protein intake, not replace it
Flavors & sweetener

Lemon and Berry flavors sweetened with stevia (Rebaudioside A) and colored with fruit and vegetable juice.

Who it's best for

People who want the full essential amino spectrum around training. Derived from the label figures above — not medical advice.

Common questions about Thorne Amino Complex

Is Thorne Amino Complex an EAA or a BCAA product?

A full-spectrum EAA product: 3.8 g of essential amino acids per serving, of which 2.5 g are the three BCAAs. Research on muscle protein synthesis centers on the full nine essential aminos rather than BCAAs alone.

Does Thorne Amino Complex disclose every dose?

Yes — the label lists an individual amount for each ingredient, with no proprietary blends. That is what the "label fully disclosed" tag on Scoop Sense means, and it is what lets each dose be compared against the amounts used in published research.
