Product details

What is Nuun Nuun Sport?

Low-calorie dissolving-tablet format: 300 mg sodium and 1 g sugar per tablet suits lighter sessions and travel-friendly dosing. It is one of the hydration mixes in the Scoop Sense database. Everything on this page comes from the manufacturer's supplement facts panel as of July 2026 — never from the marketing page.

Key ingredients & studied doses
  • Sodium · 300 mg. Sodium is the main electrolyte lost in sweat and is studied for its role in fluid balance.
  • Potassium · 150 mg. Potassium contributes to normal muscle function.
  • Magnesium · 25 mg. Magnesium plays a role in normal muscle and nerve function.
Cautions
  • 300 mg sodium per tablet still counts toward daily sodium intake
  • Talk to your doctor if you manage blood pressure or kidney conditions
Flavors & sweetener

Ten light, fizzy flavors such as Lemon Lime and Tri-Berry, sweetened with stevia and about 1 g of sugar per tablet.

Who it's best for

Lighter sessions and everyday training — the sodium load is moderate. A reasonable pick when cost per serving matters. Doses are mild enough to suit a first tub. Derived from the label figures above — not medical advice.

How much sodium is in Nuun Nuun Sport?
