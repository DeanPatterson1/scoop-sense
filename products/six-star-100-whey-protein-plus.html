Common questions about Six Star 100% Whey Protein Plus

How much of a Six Star 100% Whey Protein Plus scoop is actually protein?

30 g of protein from a 47 g serving — about 64%. The rest is flavoring, fats, carbs, and whatever else the label lists. A higher percentage means less filler per scoop.

What does the proprietary blend in Six Star 100% Whey Protein Plus hide?

The Whey Protein Plus Blend (Concentrate, Isolate, Hydrolyzed Whey) lists 30 g as a combined total. A blend total tells you the sum of its ingredients, not the individual doses inside it — which is why blend entries can't be checked against studied amounts.
