Common questions about Emergen-C Hydration+ Sports Drink Mix

How much sodium is in Emergen-C Hydration+ Sports Drink Mix?

160 mg per serving, alongside 5 g of sugar. Sodium is the main electrolyte lost in sweat, but needs vary widely with sweat rate and diet — and daily sodium from food counts toward the same total.

Does Emergen-C Hydration+ Sports Drink Mix disclose every dose?

Yes — the label lists an individual amount for each ingredient, with no proprietary blends. That is what the "label fully disclosed" tag on Scoop Sense means, and it is what lets each dose be compared against the amounts used in published research.
