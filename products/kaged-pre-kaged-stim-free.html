Common questions about Kaged Pre-Kaged Stim-Free

Is Kaged Pre-Kaged Stim-Free really caffeine-free?

The label lists 0 mg of caffeine. It is still an active formula — ingredients like L-Citrulline and BetaPower Betaine Anhydrous are dosed to have effects — so read the full label rather than treating stim-free as effect-free.

Why does Kaged Pre-Kaged Stim-Free make my skin tingle?

The 1.6 g of beta-alanine. The prickling (paresthesia) in the face, neck, and hands is generally considered harmless, is dose-dependent, and fades on its own — typically within about an hour. Most beta-alanine studies use 3.2 g per day.

Does Kaged Pre-Kaged Stim-Free disclose every dose?

Yes — the label lists an individual amount for each ingredient, with no proprietary blends. That is what the "label fully disclosed" tag on Scoop Sense means, and it is what lets each dose be compared against the amounts used in published research.
