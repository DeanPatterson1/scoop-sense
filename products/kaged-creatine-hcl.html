Product details

Where this label sits

Kaged Creatine HCl is one of the creatine products in the Scoop Sense database. It runs 75 servings per container at the full labeled serving. Everything on this page comes from the manufacturer's supplement facts panel as of July 2026 — never from the marketing page.

Key ingredients & studied doses
  • Creatine HCl · 750 mg. A hydrochloride salt of creatine studied for high water solubility, which is why the labeled serving is much smaller than a monohydrate scoop.
  • Patented creatine hydrochloride form · 750 mg. Creatine itself is studied for supporting ATP recycling during short, intense efforts; the HCl form delivers it in a roughly 1 g scoop.
Flavors & sweetener

Unflavored, Fruit Punch, and Lemon Lime; flavored versions use stevia and sucralose, and the plain powder is notably tart.

Who it's best for

People who prefer a smaller powder dose; note that HCl has a thinner research base than monohydrate. Derived from the label figures above — not medical advice.

Common questions about Kaged Creatine HCl

Do I need a loading phase with Kaged Creatine HCl?

Loading is optional. Research shows a steady 0.75 g daily serving of creatine reaches muscle saturation in about three to four weeks; a loading week of around 20 g per day just gets there faster.
