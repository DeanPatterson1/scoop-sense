Product details

Where this label sits

Gatorade Endurance Formula Powder is one of the hydration mixes in the Scoop Sense database. Every active ingredient on the panel is listed with its own dose — nothing is hidden inside a blend total. It runs 38 servings per container at the full labeled serving. Everything on this page comes from the manufacturer's supplement facts panel as of July 2026 — never from the marketing page.

Key ingredients & studied doses
  • Sodium · 300 mg. Sodium is the primary electrolyte lost in sweat and supports fluid balance and normal nerve and muscle function.
  • Potassium · 140 mg. Potassium works alongside sodium to help maintain fluid balance inside and outside cells.
Flavors & sweetener

Who it's best for

Everyday training and moderate sweat losses — a middling sodium load. The sugar is part of the design, aiding fluid uptake the way oral rehydration research uses glucose — skip it if you want zero sugar. Derived from the label figures above — not medical advice.

Common questions about Gatorade Endurance Formula Powder

How much sodium is in Gatorade Endurance Formula Powder?

300 mg per serving, alongside 13 g of sugar. Sodium is the main electrolyte lost in sweat, but needs vary widely with sweat rate and diet — and daily sodium from food counts toward the same total.

Does Gatorade Endurance Formula Powder disclose every dose?

Yes — the label lists an individual amount for each ingredient, with no proprietary blends. That is what the "label fully disclosed" tag on Scoop Sense means, and it is what lets each dose be compared against the amounts used in published research.
