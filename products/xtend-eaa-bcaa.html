Product details

Where this label sits

Xtend EAA is one of the amino formulas in the Scoop Sense database. The label uses at least one proprietary blend, so a blend total stands in place of the individual amounts inside it. It runs 40 servings per container at the full labeled serving. Everything on this page comes from the manufacturer's supplement facts panel as of September 2026 — never from the marketing page.

Key ingredients & studied doses
  • XTEND EAA Amino Blend · 5 g. Covers all nine essential amino acids, but the per-scoop total sits under the roughly 10 to 15 g used in most free-form EAA research — the label's own two-scoop direction is what reaches 10 g.
  • L-Leucine · 1.5 g. Below the roughly 2.5 to 3 g leucine amount commonly used to trigger muscle protein synthesis in studies; two scoops reach 3 g.
  • Electrolytes (sodium, potassium) · 150 mg sodium / 120 mg potassium. A light electrolyte addition for sipping during training, far below the amounts in dedicated hydration mixes.
Flavors & sweetener

Four flavors — Mango, Lemon Lime, Blood Orange and Watermelon — sugar-free and sweetened with sucralose plus acesulfame potassium; the 328 g net weight is the Mango tub's, and the others vary a little.

Who it's best for

People who want the full essential amino spectrum around training. Skip it if you want every individual dose disclosed on the label. Derived from the label figures above — not medical advice.

Common questions about Xtend EAA

Is Xtend EAA an EAA or a BCAA product?

A full-spectrum EAA product: 5 g of essential amino acids per serving, of which 3 g are the three BCAAs. Research on muscle protein synthesis centers on the full nine essential aminos rather than BCAAs alone.

What does the proprietary blend in Xtend EAA hide?

The XTEND EAA Amino Blend lists 5 g as a combined total. A blend total tells you the sum of its ingredients, not the individual doses inside it — which is why blend entries can't be checked against studied amounts.

Do the numbers for Xtend EAA assume one scoop or two?

All figures on this page are per full labeled serving. The 10 g EAA figure on the front of the tub is a two-serving number; one scoop supplies 5 g.
