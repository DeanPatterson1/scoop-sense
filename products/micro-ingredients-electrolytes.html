Product details

Where this label sits

Micro Ingredients Sport Electrolytes Powder is one of the hydration mixes in the Scoop Sense database. Every active ingredient on the panel is listed with its own dose — nothing is hidden inside a blend total. It runs 90 servings per container at the full labeled serving. Everything on this page comes from the manufacturer's supplement facts panel as of September 2026 — never from the marketing page.

Key ingredients & studied doses
  • Sodium (sodium chloride) · 500 mg. Sodium is the electrolyte lost in the greatest quantity in sweat, and 500 mg per serving sits in the range sports-drink research uses for replacing a hard training session's losses.
  • Potassium (potassium citrate) · 400 mg. About 9 percent of a day's Daily Value; potassium works alongside sodium in normal fluid balance and muscle function, and sweat carries far less of it than sodium.
  • Magnesium (magnesium citrate) · 100 mg. 24 percent of a day's Daily Value, and at the upper end of what hydration mixes usually carry; magnesium plays a role in normal muscle and nerve function.
  • beta-alanine · 500 mg. Well under the roughly 3.2 to 6.4 g per day used in beta-alanine research, where the effect comes from muscle carnosine built up over weeks of daily dosing rather than from any single serving.
Flavors & sweetener

Four flavors in the 2 lb bag, Watermelon Lime, Berry Punch, Passionfruit & Citrus and Piña Colada, sweetened with stevia leaf extract rather than sucralose; serving counts vary a little by flavor, 90 for Watermelon Lime and Berry Punch against 95 for Passionfruit & Citrus, and the panel figures here are the Watermelon Lime bag's.

Who it's best for

Everyday training and moderate sweat losses — a middling sodium load. Derived from the label figures above — not medical advice.

Common questions about Micro Ingredients Sport Electrolytes Powder

How much sodium is in Micro Ingredients Sport Electrolytes Powder?

500 mg per serving, with no sugar. Sodium is the main electrolyte lost in sweat, but needs vary widely with sweat rate and diet — and daily sodium from food counts toward the same total.

Why does Micro Ingredients Sport Electrolytes Powder make my skin tingle?

The 500 mg of beta-alanine. The prickling (paresthesia) in the face, neck, and hands is generally considered harmless, is dose-dependent, and fades on its own — typically within about an hour. Most beta-alanine studies use 3.2 g per day.

Does Micro Ingredients Sport Electrolytes Powder disclose every dose?

Yes — the label lists an individual amount for each ingredient, with no proprietary blends. That is what the "label fully disclosed" tag on Scoop Sense means, and it is what lets each dose be compared against the amounts used in published research.

Do the numbers for Micro Ingredients Sport Electrolytes Powder assume one scoop or two?

All figures on this page are per full labeled serving. A serving is two scoops, about 10 g, so a single scoop delivers half of every number on the panel.
