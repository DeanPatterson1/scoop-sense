Key ingredients & studied doses
  • Sodium (sodium chloride) · 110 mg. Sodium is the primary electrolyte lost in sweat and is studied for its role in fluid balance.
  • Potassium (potassium aspartate) · 250 mg. Potassium works alongside sodium to support normal muscle and nerve function.
  • Magnesium (magnesium citrate) · 100 mg. Provides 25% of the Daily Value; magnesium supports normal muscle relaxation and energy metabolism.
  • Zinc (zinc citrate) · 2.9 mg. Zinc contributes to normal immune function alongside the electrolyte blend.

Common questions about Key Nutrients Electrolyte Recovery Plus

How much sodium is in Key Nutrients Electrolyte Recovery Plus?

110 mg per serving, with no sugar. Sodium is the main electrolyte lost in sweat, but needs vary widely with sweat rate and diet — and daily sodium from food counts toward the same total.

Does Key Nutrients Electrolyte Recovery Plus disclose every dose?

Yes — the label lists an individual amount for each ingredient, with no proprietary blends. That is what the "label fully disclosed" tag on Scoop Sense means, and it is what lets each dose be compared against the amounts used in published research.
