Common questions about Muscle Milk Genuine Protein Powder

How much of a Muscle Milk Genuine Protein Powder scoop is actually protein?

32 g of protein from a 70 g serving — about 46%. The rest is flavoring, fats, carbs, and whatever else the label lists. A higher percentage means less filler per scoop.

What does the proprietary blend in Muscle Milk Genuine Protein Powder hide?

The Milk Protein Blend (Calcium Sodium Caseinate, Milk Protein Isolate, Whey Isolate, Whey Hydrolysate, Whey Concentrate) lists 32 g as a combined total. A blend total tells you the sum of its ingredients, not the individual doses inside it — which is why blend entries can't be checked against studied amounts.
