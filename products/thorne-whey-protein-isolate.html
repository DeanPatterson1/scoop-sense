  • Whey Protein Isolate · 25 g (providing 21 g protein). Complete, fast-digesting dairy protein studied for its role in supporting muscle protein synthesis after resistance training.

Common questions about Thorne Whey Protein Isolate

How much of a Thorne Whey Protein Isolate scoop is actually protein?

21 g of protein from a 28 g serving — about 75%. The rest is flavoring, fats, carbs, and whatever else the label lists. A higher percentage means less filler per scoop.

What does the proprietary blend in Thorne Whey Protein Isolate hide?

The Bromelain and Papain Blend lists 30 mg as a combined total. A blend total tells you the sum of its ingredients, not the individual doses inside it — which is why blend entries can't be checked against studied amounts.
