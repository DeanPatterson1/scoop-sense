Key ingredients & studied doses
  • Vegan BCAAs (Leucine, Isoleucine, Valine) · 5 g. Fermented, instantized branched-chain aminos in a 2:1:1 ratio studied for supporting muscle recovery.
  • L-Glutamine · 3 g. The most abundant free amino acid in muscle tissue, commonly added to recovery formulas.
  • Citicoline (as Cognizin) + Rhodiola rosea extract · 250 mg + 200 mg. Studied for supporting mental focus and reducing perceived fatigue during exercise.
  • Caffeine (from Coffea arabica bean extract) · 100 mg. A moderate dose studied for supporting alertness and perceived energy during training.

Common questions about Genius Energized BCAA

How much caffeine is in Genius Energized BCAA?

100 mg per full labeled serving — roughly 1.1 small cups of coffee. The FDA cites about 400 mg per day as an amount generally not associated with negative effects in healthy adults, and coffee, tea, and energy drinks count toward the same total.

Is Genius Energized BCAA an EAA or a BCAA product?

BCAA-only: it supplies the three branched-chain aminos (5 g per serving) but not the other six essential amino acids that full-EAA formulas include.

Does Genius Energized BCAA disclose every dose?

Yes — the label lists an individual amount for each ingredient, with no proprietary blends. That is what the "label fully disclosed" tag on Scoop Sense means, and it is what lets each dose be compared against the amounts used in published research.
