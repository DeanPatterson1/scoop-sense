Product details

Where this label sits

Vitalyte Electrolyte Replacement Drink Mix is one of the hydration mixes in the Scoop Sense database. Every active ingredient on the panel is listed with its own dose — nothing is hidden inside a blend total. It runs 40 servings per container at the full labeled serving. Everything on this page comes from the manufacturer's supplement facts panel as of July 2026 — never from the marketing page.

Key ingredients & studied doses
  • Sodium · 135 mg. Sodium supports normal fluid balance and is lost through sweat during exercise.
  • Potassium · 193 mg. Potassium supports normal muscle and nerve function alongside sodium.
  • Magnesium · 3 mg. Magnesium is present in a minor amount here compared with the sodium and potassium doses.
Flavors & sweetener

Available in Orange, Lemon, Grape, Fruit Punch, and Cool Citrus, sweetened primarily with glucose and fructose rather than artificial sweeteners.

Who it's best for

Flavour and light top-ups rather than replacing a real sweat loss — the sodium here is well under the amount hydration research works with. The sugar is part of the design, aiding fluid uptake the way oral rehydration research uses glucose — skip it if you want zero sugar. Derived from the label figures above — not medical advice.

Common questions about Vitalyte Electrolyte Replacement Drink Mix

How much sodium is in Vitalyte Electrolyte Replacement Drink Mix?

135 mg per serving, alongside 21 g of sugar. Sodium is the main electrolyte lost in sweat, but needs vary widely with sweat rate and diet — and daily sodium from food counts toward the same total.

Does Vitalyte Electrolyte Replacement Drink Mix disclose every dose?

Yes — the label lists an individual amount for each ingredient, with no proprietary blends. That is what the "label fully disclosed" tag on Scoop Sense means, and it is what lets each dose be compared against the amounts used in published research.
