Product details

Where this label sits

Alani Nu BCAA is one of the amino formulas in the Scoop Sense database. The label uses at least one proprietary blend, so a blend total stands in place of the individual amounts inside it. It runs 30 servings per container at the full labeled serving. Everything on this page comes from the manufacturer's supplement facts panel as of July 2026 — never from the marketing page.

Key ingredients & studied doses
  • BCAA 2:1:1 blend · 5 g. Branched-chain aminos studied for supporting muscle recovery, with 2.5 g of that total as leucine.
  • L-Glutamine · 500 mg. The most abundant free amino acid in muscle tissue, commonly added to recovery formulas.
  • Electrolytes (sodium, potassium) · 84 mg combined. Minerals lost through sweat, added to support hydration.

Who it's best for

Common questions about Alani Nu BCAA

Is Alani Nu BCAA an EAA or a BCAA product?

BCAA-only: it supplies the three branched-chain aminos (5 g per serving) but not the other six essential amino acids that full-EAA formulas include.

What does the proprietary blend in Alani Nu BCAA hide?

The BCAA 2:1:1 blend lists 5 g as a combined total. A blend total tells you the sum of its ingredients, not the individual doses inside it — which is why blend entries can't be checked against studied amounts.
