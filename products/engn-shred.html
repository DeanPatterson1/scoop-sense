Key ingredients & studied doses
  • Beta-Alanine · 1.6 g. Below the 3.2g+ doses used in most carnosine-loading studies; likely to produce a milder tingling effect than a full dose.
  • L-Carnitine L-Tartrate · 500 mg. Matches doses used in some exercise-recovery research, though oral carnitine's fat-oxidation evidence in trained individuals is mixed.
  • Yohimbe Extract · 40 mg. Yohimbine content isn't standardized on this label; combined with the caffeine blend, this adds meaningfully to the overall stimulant load.
  • Red Pepper Fruit Extract (Capsimax) · 25 mg. A standardized capsaicinoid dose in line with amounts used in thermogenic research.

Common questions about Evlution Nutrition ENGN Shred

How much caffeine is in Evlution Nutrition ENGN Shred?

260 mg per full labeled serving — roughly 2.7 small cups of coffee. Within the Scoop Sense pre-workout database (0–400 mg per serving) that places it in the high stim tier. The FDA cites about 400 mg per day as an amount generally not associated with negative effects in healthy adults, and coffee, tea, and energy drinks count toward the same total.

Why does Evlution Nutrition ENGN Shred make my skin tingle?

The 1.6 g of beta-alanine. The prickling (paresthesia) in the face, neck, and hands is generally considered harmless, is dose-dependent, and fades on its own — typically within about an hour. Most beta-alanine studies use 3.2 g per day.

What does the proprietary blend in Evlution Nutrition ENGN Shred hide?

The blend lists one combined total. A blend total tells you the sum of its ingredients, not the individual doses inside it — which is why blend entries can't be checked against studied amounts.

Do the numbers for Evlution Nutrition ENGN Shred assume one scoop or two?
