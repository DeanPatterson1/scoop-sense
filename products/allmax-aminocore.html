Product details

Where this label sits

ALLMAX Aminocore is one of the amino formulas in the Scoop Sense database. Every active ingredient on the panel is listed with its own dose — nothing is hidden inside a blend total. It runs 30 servings per container at the full labeled serving. Everything on this page comes from the manufacturer's supplement facts panel as of July 2026 — never from the marketing page.

Key ingredients & studied doses
  • L-Leucine · 3.68 g. The BCAA most studied for triggering muscle protein synthesis, dosed here at 45% of the blend.
  • L-Valine · 2.45 g. One of the two remaining BCAAs, dosed at 30% of the blend.
  • L-Isoleucine · 2.05 g. Completes the leucine-heavy 9:6:5 BCAA ratio at 25% of the blend.
  • B-vitamin complex (B6, B12, folate, niacin) · included per serving. Cofactors involved in normal amino acid metabolism.

Common questions about ALLMAX Aminocore

Is ALLMAX Aminocore an EAA or a BCAA product?

BCAA-only: it supplies the three branched-chain aminos (8.18 g per serving) but not the other six essential amino acids that full-EAA formulas include.

Does ALLMAX Aminocore disclose every dose?

Yes — the label lists an individual amount for each ingredient, with no proprietary blends. That is what the "label fully disclosed" tag on Scoop Sense means, and it is what lets each dose be compared against the amounts used in published research.
