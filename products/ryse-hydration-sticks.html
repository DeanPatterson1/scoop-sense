Common questions about RYSE Hydration Sticks

How much sodium is in RYSE Hydration Sticks?

320 mg per serving, with no sugar. Sodium is the main electrolyte lost in sweat, but needs vary widely with sweat rate and diet — and daily sodium from food counts toward the same total.

Does RYSE Hydration Sticks disclose every dose?

Yes — the label lists an individual amount for each ingredient, with no proprietary blends. That is what the "label fully disclosed" tag on Scoop Sense means, and it is what lets each dose be compared against the amounts used in published research.
