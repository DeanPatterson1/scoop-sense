Product details

Where this label sits

Animal Fury sits in the high stim tier of the Scoop Sense pre-workout database, which spans 0–400 mg of caffeine per serving. The label uses at least one proprietary blend, so a blend total stands in place of the individual amounts inside it. It runs 30 servings per container at the full labeled serving. Everything on this page comes from the manufacturer's supplement facts panel as of September 2026 — never from the marketing page.

Key ingredients & studied doses
  • Citrulline Malate · 6 g. At the bottom of the 6-8g range used in most citrulline malate studies, which works out to roughly 4g of actual citrulline.
  • Caffeine Anhydrous · 350 mg. Well above the ~200mg single dose used in most caffeine performance research, and close to the FDA's ~400mg daily reference for healthy adults.
  • Instant BCAA Blend (Leucine, Isoleucine, Valine) · 5 g. 5g is a conventional BCAA serving, but the effects studied in BCAA research track the leucine portion, and this pooled total does not say how much of the 5g is leucine.
  • Beta-Alanine · 2 g. Under the 3.2g daily amount used in most beta-alanine carnosine research, so this is a partial daily dose rather than a full one.
Flavors & sweetener

Seven flavors (Blue Raspberry, Green Apple, Kiwi Lime, Ice Pop, Watermelon, Fruit Punch, Orange) in 20- and 30-serving tubs, sweetened with sucralose and acesulfame potassium; the Blue Raspberry panel checked here takes its color from spirulina extract, while older flavor panels list FD&C dyes.

Common questions about Animal Fury

How much caffeine is in Animal Fury?

350 mg per full labeled serving — roughly 3.7 small cups of coffee. Within the Scoop Sense pre-workout database (0–400 mg per serving) that places it in the high stim tier. The FDA cites about 400 mg per day as an amount generally not associated with negative effects in healthy adults, and coffee, tea, and energy drinks count toward the same total.

Why does Animal Fury make my skin tingle?

The 2 g of beta-alanine. The prickling (paresthesia) in the face, neck, and hands is generally considered harmless, is dose-dependent, and fades on its own — typically within about an hour. Most beta-alanine studies use 3.2 g per day.

What does the proprietary blend in Animal Fury hide?

The Instant BCAA Blend (Leucine, Isoleucine, Valine) lists 5 g as a combined total. A blend total tells you the sum of its ingredients, not the individual doses inside it — which is why blend entries can't be checked against studied amounts.

Do the numbers for Animal Fury assume one scoop or two?
