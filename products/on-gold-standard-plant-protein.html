Product details

Where this label sits

Optimum Nutrition Gold Standard 100% Plant Protein is one of the protein powders in the Scoop Sense database. It runs 12 servings per container at the full labeled serving. Everything on this page comes from the manufacturer's supplement facts panel as of July 2026 — never from the marketing page.

Key ingredients & studied doses
  • Plant Protein Blend (pea protein, fava bean protein isolate, brown rice protein concentrate) · 24 g. A three-source legume/grain blend; pea and rice proteins are the most studied of the three for supporting muscle protein synthesis.
  • Oil Creamer (coconut/sunflower oil, pea protein, sunflower lecithin) · amount not stated on label. Added for mouthfeel; contributes to the panel's fat content.
  • Gum Blend (xanthan gum, guar gum, gum acacia) · amount not stated on label. Texture and thickening agents, standard in plant-protein shakes.
Flavors & sweetener

Figures verified against Creamy Vanilla (37 g scoop, 0.98 lb/12-serving tub); sweetened with stevia leaf extract, no artificial sweeteners.

Who it's best for

A standard-ratio powder — fine as a food supplement when total daily protein is what matters. Suits anyone avoiding dairy. Doses are mild enough to suit a first tub. Derived from the label figures above — not medical advice.
