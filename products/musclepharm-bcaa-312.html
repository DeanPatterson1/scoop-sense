Product details

What is MusclePharm BCAA 3:1:2?

Budget classic BCAA using an unconventional 3:1:2 ratio that weights valine over isoleucine; like all BCAA-only formulas it omits the six other essential aminos. It is one of the amino formulas in the Scoop Sense database. Everything on this page comes from the manufacturer's supplement facts panel as of July 2026 — never from the marketing page.

Key ingredients & studied doses
  • L-Leucine · 3 g. The essential amino acid most studied for stimulating muscle protein synthesis.
  • L-Valine · 2 g. Branched-chain amino acid weighted above isoleucine in this formula's 3:1:2 ratio.
  • L-Isoleucine · 1 g. Branched-chain amino acid involved in muscle energy metabolism.
Cautions
  • BCAA-only formulas lack six of the nine essential amino acids
  • Amino drinks supplement protein intake, not replace it
Flavors & sweetener

Blue Raspberry and Fruit Punch flavors sweetened with sucralose.

Who it's best for

A BCAA-only formula — three of the nine essential amino acids, which suits taste-driven intra-workout sipping more than protein replacement. Derived from the label figures above — not medical advice.

Common questions about MusclePharm BCAA 3:1:2

Is MusclePharm BCAA 3:1:2 an EAA or a BCAA product?

BCAA-only: it supplies the three branched-chain aminos (6 g per serving) but not the other six essential amino acids that full-EAA formulas include.

Does MusclePharm BCAA 3:1:2 disclose every dose?

Yes — the label lists an individual amount for each ingredient, with no proprietary blends. That is what the "label fully disclosed" tag on Scoop Sense means, and it is what lets each dose be compared against the amounts used in published research.
