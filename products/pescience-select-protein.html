Product details

Where this label sits

PEScience Select Protein is one of the protein powders in the Scoop Sense database. The label uses at least one proprietary blend, so a blend total stands in place of the individual amounts inside it. It runs 27 servings per container at the full labeled serving. Everything on this page comes from the manufacturer's supplement facts panel as of September 2026 — never from the marketing page.

Key ingredients & studied doses
  • Select Protein Blend (milk protein isolate [casein protein and whey protein], whey protein concentrate 80%, leucine peptides) · 24 g. Milk protein isolate is listed first, and milk protein is naturally about 80 percent casein, so this is a slow-plus-fast dairy blend rather than straight whey; the 24 g total sits inside the 20-40 g per-serving range used in muscle protein synthesis research.
  • Leucine peptides · amount not stated on label. Leucine is the amino acid most studied for triggering muscle protein synthesis, but the label prints no weight for the added peptides, so the added portion cannot be compared to research doses.
  • Sucralose and acesulfame potassium · amount not stated on label. The two high-intensity sweeteners on every flavored panel; the Unflavored version is the one ingredient list that carries neither, and it also drops the leucine peptides.

Common questions about PEScience Select Protein

How much of a PEScience Select Protein scoop is actually protein?

24 g of protein from a 32 g serving — about 75%. The rest is flavoring, fats, carbs, and whatever else the label lists. A higher percentage means less filler per scoop.

What does the proprietary blend in PEScience Select Protein hide?

The Select Protein Blend (milk protein isolate [casein protein and whey protein], whey protein concentrate 80%, leucine peptides) lists 24 g as a combined total. A blend total tells you the sum of its ingredients, not the individual doses inside it — which is why blend entries can't be checked against studied amounts.
