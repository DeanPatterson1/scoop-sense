Product details

What is MusclePharm Assault?

A fully-disclosed, no-blend budget pre-workout at roughly a dollar a serving or less, pairing a moderate-high 250mg caffeine dose with 3g creatine in an 11.5g scoop, though its beta-alanine and betaine doses sit toward the lower end of what's typically studied. It sits in the high stim tier of the Scoop Sense pre-workout database, which spans 0–400 mg of caffeine per serving. Everything on this page comes from the manufacturer's supplement facts panel as of July 2026 — never from the marketing page.

Key ingredients & studied doses
  • Caffeine Anhydrous · 250 mg. Comparable to about 2.5 cups of brewed coffee; within the range commonly used in acute exercise-performance caffeine studies.
  • Creatine Monohydrate · 3 g. Matches the standard 3-5g daily maintenance dose used in creatine research.
  • Beta-Alanine (CarnoSyn) · 1.75 g. Below the ~3.2g daily split-dose typically used in studies on raising muscle carnosine, though sourced as branded CarnoSyn.
  • Betaine Anhydrous · 1.5 g. At the low end of the 1.25-2.5g range used in betaine and power-output research.
Cautions
  • 250mg caffeine per scoop is roughly 2.5 cups of coffee.
  • 1.75g beta-alanine may cause a temporary tingling sensation.
  • The lower per-serving cost likely reflects lighter dosing on beta-alanine and betaine, plus no citrulline at all, versus premium-tier fully-dosed pre-workouts.
Flavors & sweetener

Comes in fruit-candy flavors like Blue Raspberry, Fruit Punch, Watermelon, and Melon Hwachae, sweetened with sucralose and acesulfame potassium.

Who it's best for

Users with an established caffeine tolerance. A reasonable pick when cost per serving matters. Derived from the label figures above — not medical advice.

Common questions about MusclePharm Assault

How much caffeine is in MusclePharm Assault?

250 mg per full labeled serving — roughly 2.6 small cups of coffee. Within the Scoop Sense pre-workout database (0–400 mg per serving) that places it in the high stim tier. The FDA cites about 400 mg per day as an amount generally not associated with negative effects in healthy adults, and coffee, tea, and energy drinks count toward the same total.

Why does MusclePharm Assault make my skin tingle?

The 1.75 g of beta-alanine. The prickling (paresthesia) in the face, neck, and hands is generally considered harmless, is dose-dependent, and fades on its own — typically within about an hour. Most beta-alanine studies use 3.2 g per day.

Does MusclePharm Assault disclose every dose?

Yes — the label lists an individual amount for each ingredient, with no proprietary blends. That is what the "label fully disclosed" tag on Scoop Sense means, and it is what lets each dose be compared against the amounts used in published research.
