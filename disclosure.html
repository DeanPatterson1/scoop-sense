How we make money

Last updated: July 2026

The short version

Some links on Scoop Sense are affiliate links. If you click one and buy something, the retailer pays us a small commission. You pay exactly the same price either way.

What that means in practice

When you see a button like "Check price on Amazon," that link carries a tracking tag. If you make a purchase after clicking it, we earn a percentage of the sale from the retailer — not from you. This is the only way this site makes money. We don't run ads, sell your data, or charge for anything.

What commissions never buy

  • Placement — no brand can pay to be listed, featured, or ranked.
  • Silence — cautions and label criticisms stay up whether or not a product earns us commission.
  • Spin — the doses and caffeine numbers come from the label, and we report them the same way for every product.

Amazon Associates

As an Amazon Associate, Scoop Sense earns from qualifying purchases.

Prices and availability shown on Amazon are accurate as of the time you visit Amazon; we deliberately do not display prices here because they change constantly.

Why we tell you this

Two reasons. The Federal Trade Commission requires sites like ours to disclose affiliate relationships clearly and close to the links themselves — which is why you'll see a short disclosure line near every buy button, not just on this page. And more simply: you should never have to wonder how the site you're trusting gets paid.
